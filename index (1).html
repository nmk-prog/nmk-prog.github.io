Why do people trust some messages, especially AI answers that sound confident and professional?

I'm Md. Nafiz Mahmud Khan, a recent graduate in Business and Technology Management from the Islamic University of Technology (IUT) in Dhaka, Bangladesh. That simple but important question sits at the centre of my work.

Through my research, I have used surveys, regression, mediation analysis and open-ended responses to study how people use AI in their work and decisions. Now I want to go a step further and understand what people actually do when they receive AI-generated information, not just what they say about it.
